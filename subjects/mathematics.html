📝 Essential Math Formulas

Algebra

  • Quadratic Formula: x = (-b ± √(b²-4ac))/2a
  • Laws of Indices: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
  • Difference of Squares: a² - b² = (a+b)(a-b)
  • Binomial Theorem: (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Geometry

  • Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²
  • Area of Circle: A = πr²
  • Circumference: C = 2πr
  • Volume of Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³

Trigonometry

  • SOH-CAH-TOA
  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
  • Sine Rule: a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC
  • Cosine Rule: a² = b² + c² - 2bc cosA

Calculus

  • Power Rule: d/dx(xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹
  • Chain Rule: dy/dx = dy/du × du/dx
  • Integration: ∫xⁿ dx = (xⁿ⁺¹)/(n+1) + C
  • Area under curve: ∫f(x) dx

Statistics

  • Mean: Σx/n
  • Variance: Σ(x-μ)²/n
  • Standard Deviation: √Variance
  • Z-score: (x-μ)/σ

Coordinate Geometry

  • Distance: √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]
  • Midpoint: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
  • Gradient: (y₂-y₁)/(x₂-x₁)
  • Equation: y = mx + c
