📊 Essential Accounting Formulas

Financial Statements

  • Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity
  • Net Income = Revenue - Expenses
  • Gross Profit = Sales - COGS
  • Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities
  • Retained Earnings = Beginning RE + Net Income - Dividends

Key Ratios

  • Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
  • Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
  • Debt-to-Equity = Total Liabilities / Shareholder's Equity
  • Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue
  • ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100%

Cost Accounting

  • COGS = Opening Stock + Purchases - Closing Stock
  • Break-even Point = Fixed Costs / (Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit)
  • Contribution Margin = Sales - Variable Costs
  • Markup % = (Selling Price - Cost) / Cost × 100%
  • Margin % = (Selling Price - Cost) / Selling Price × 100%

Depreciation

  • Straight-line = (Cost - Salvage Value) / Useful Life
  • Reducing Balance = Book Value × Rate
  • Units of Production = (Cost - Salvage) × (Units This Period / Total Estimated Units)
  • Double Declining = 2 × (1/Useful Life) × Book Value
  • Sum of Years' Digits = (Remaining Life / SYD) × (Cost - Salvage)

Financial Analysis

  • EPS = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Avg. Outstanding Shares
  • P/E Ratio = Price per Share / EPS
  • Inventory Turnover = COGS / Avg. Inventory
  • Accounts Receivable Turnover = Net Credit Sales / Avg. Accounts Receivable

Budgeting & Forecasting

  • Cash Flow Forecast = Beginning Cash + Projected Inflows - Projected Outflows
  • Sales Forecast = Previous Sales × (1 + Growth Rate)
  • Production Budget = Budgeted Sales + Desired Ending Inventory - Beginning Inventory
  • Direct Materials Budget = (Units to be Produced × Material per Unit) + Desired Ending Material - Beginning Material
